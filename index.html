FAQ

Questions people actually ask

What counts as a duplicate field?

Two or more custom fields whose names match once case and extra whitespace are ignored — "Story Points" and "story  points" are treated as the same name. Near-duplicates like "Story Point" vs "Story Points" are flagged separately, as possible duplicates.

Does Field Hygiene delete or merge fields?

No. It's read-only — it reports what it finds, and you decide what to do about it directly in Jira. It never renames, merges, or deletes anything.

What does "unused" actually mean?

A field that isn't attached to any screen, or one Jira has tracked as untouched for two or more years. Both are candidates for cleanup, not automatically safe to delete.

What data does it store?

Only a daily aggregate snapshot for the Trends chart — four counts, never a field name, ID, or any other identifying content. See the privacy policy for detail.

Is it free?

Yes, for sites of 10 users or fewer. Paid per seat above that, with the same features at every tier.

How do I export a scan?

Click Export, then select all the text in the box that appears and copy it — paste it into a ticket, a spreadsheet, or a message.
