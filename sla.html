Support & SLA

Last updated: 7 August 2026
Partner supported · support@isogunlabs.com

Field Hygiene for Jira is built and supported by Isogun Labs, an independent studio — not a large vendor with a dedicated support team, but a direct line to the person who built the app. This page states plainly what to expect.

How to reach us

Email support@isogunlabs.com for any question, bug report, or issue with Field Hygiene. This is the same address used for security reports (see the Security Policy) and general product questions — one inbox, read directly by the vendor.

Hours of operation

Support is available Monday through Friday, during standard business hours. We do not currently offer weekend or holiday coverage.

Response-time commitment

We aim to respond to every support request within one business day of receipt. This is a response-time commitment, not a resolution-time guarantee — some issues take longer to fully resolve, but you will hear back from us within one business day acknowledging the request and outlining next steps.

Scope

This commitment covers Field Hygiene for Jira specifically. It does not cover the underlying availability of Atlassian Jira or Atlassian Forge itself, which is governed by Atlassian's own platform SLAs — Field Hygiene has no infrastructure of its own separate from Forge to offer an independent uptime guarantee for.
